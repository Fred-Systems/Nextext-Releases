### What is NexText?
NexText is a fully loaded, hyper-customizable modern messaging app designed exclusively for NexText users. 

### 🌟 Key Features:
* **Rich Messaging:** Core support for private chats, group conversations, and status updates.
* **Ultimate Privacy (Disguise Mode):** A built-in app icon picker that lets you disguise the application as a Calculator or a Notepad on your device.
* **Advanced AI Capabilities:** 
  * Smart NexText AI Chatbot (available upon request).
  * Real-time audio transcription and voice-to-text functionality.
  * Instant language translation.
* **Modern UI:** Complete control over your chat interface with deep visual customization.
